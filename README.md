# My Gym Routine

## Why I Enjoy Going to the Gym

Going to the gym is one of my **favorite parts of the day**. I enjoy it because I want to be *strong and healthy* and it's also a good way to take a break from studying :D.

### My Weekly Workout Schedule

I try to go to the gym ***three or four times a week*** and each day I work on a different muscle area. This helps me to give my muscles time to recover.

I usually divide it into:

- legs
- back and biceps
- chest, shoulders and triceps
- abs

| Day | Muscle Group |
|---|---|
| Monday | Legs |
| Tuesday | Back and biceps |
| Thursday | Chest, shoulders and triceps |
| Saturday | Abs |

### How i Organize a Workout

1. I start with a warm-up.
2. I do some cardio.
3. I continue with some strength exercises.
4. I finish with stretching.

## A Good Fitness Website

If I want to try something new but I don't know what to do, I usually look online for some insipration, [Muscle&Strenght](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises) is an example.
